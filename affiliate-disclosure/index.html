Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: August 13, 2026

I want you to know when a link can benefit me financially. The relevant relationships on this site are explained below and are also disclosed close to the links themselves.

Carrd referral links

Some Carrd links on this site—including public build links for my free templates—activate my Carrd referral code. If you later purchase an eligible Carrd Pro upgrade or renewal, I may receive Carrd account credit equal to 30% of the amount paid toward that eligible transaction. Your price is unchanged.

Carrd Maker Program

ShipToday is sold through Carrd’s Maker Program. I am the template creator and receive revenue from each purchase. Carrd handles the transaction and access to the template. Carrd is a separate company and does not sponsor or approve every statement on this site.

Payhip Partner Program

Some Payhip links on this site are partner links. If you create a qualifying account through one of these links, I may receive 50% of the platform revenue Payhip earns from that account, recurring, at no extra cost to you. This percentage applies to Payhip’s own revenue—not to the seller’s gross sales. I am an independent partner, not a Payhip employee or representative. Self-referrals are not permitted.

Free templates and optional tips

Byline and Northstar Ops are genuinely free. Nothing is locked behind a payment. Their creator-support blocks can be deleted before publishing. A PayPal tip is optional, does not purchase the template, and unlocks no additional content or service.

Editorial standard

I do not recommend a product only because it offers a referral benefit. I aim to describe costs, limits, and commercial relationships clearly. If a relationship changes, I will update this page and the relevant disclosure.
