About Frank

Built by the sea. Guided by systems.

I grew up in the Islas del Rosario near Cartagena and trained in naval electromechanics. That background taught me to respect materials, systems, and the small details that become large problems when ignored.

Today I apply the same thinking as founder of Colombia Boat Detailing and as an independent web creator: understand the real problem, remove what does not help, and build something useful.

This site is where I share the ventures, templates, and lessons I am building from Colombia for a wider audience.
