Profile

A technical foundation, turned into useful work.

I grew up in the Islas del Rosario near Cartagena and trained in naval electromechanics. Life around boats taught me to notice materials, systems, corrosion, maintenance, and the operational details that are easy to overlook from shore.

I founded Colombia Boat Detailing to apply that perspective to professional yacht and boat care in Cartagena. I shaped its positioning, service system, digital presence, and customer journey around real conditions in the Caribbean marine environment.

Alongside the marine business, I create original Carrd templates and focused websites for founders and service businesses. The common thread is simple: clarify the real problem, build a reliable system, and make the next step obvious.
