Seven-step setup

Build the product before building the promotion.

A useful product, a clear license, and an honest sales page matter more than publishing on every channel at once.

  1. Define one buyer and one result

    Write down who the product is for, the problem it solves, what the buyer receives, and what it does not include.

  2. Prepare the final delivery package

    Organize the files, instructions, license, version number, contact route, and a short README. Open every file before uploading it.

  3. Create the Payhip storefront

    Choose a clear store name, complete the profile, and start on the Free plan unless your expected volume justifies a paid tier.

  4. Connect an available payment gateway

    Use the payment options shown for your country and account. Review each processor’s own fees before going live.

  5. Create the product page

    Add a specific title, original preview images, benefits, file details, support terms, refund information, and a price that matches the scope.

  6. Connect the Carrd purchase button

    Link the primary button on your Carrd sales page to the matching Payhip product or checkout route. Keep the action consistent across the page.

  7. Test the complete buyer journey

    Check the page on mobile, verify every link, preview checkout, review the receipt and delivery settings, and confirm the product package is final.

Payhip vs Gumroad

Lower platform fees or broader tax handling?

Neither platform wins every situation. Payhip can cost less when you bring your own audience. Gumroad’s Merchant of Record model can reduce the operational burden of indirect tax collection worldwide. Compare the whole workflow, not only the headline percentage.

Common questions

Know the boundaries before you launch.

Can I start selling on Payhip for free?

Yes. Payhip’s Free Forever plan has no monthly subscription and charges a 5% platform transaction fee. Payment processor fees still apply.

Can I connect Payhip to a Carrd website?

Yes. Use Carrd as the sales page and link its purchase buttons to the relevant Payhip product or checkout page. Label the button clearly so buyers know they are moving to checkout.

Should I sell a Carrd template itself through Payhip?

Carrd provides its Maker Program as the official route for selling Carrd templates. Payhip is better used for separate digital products such as guides, asset packs, courses, and downloads.

Does Frank Mercado’s Payhip partner link cost me more?

No. The price and plan terms are the same. I may receive a recurring commission from Payhip’s platform revenue if the referred account qualifies.

Does Payhip handle every seller tax automatically?

Payhip states that it automatically handles EU VAT and UK VAT and provides settings for other taxes. Sellers remain responsible for checking the obligations that apply to their business and country.
